Jasmine Lara Saldano is a designer and developer working between Davis and Los Angeles. Lara Studio approaches each project as a conversation between how something works and how it feels, combining code, research, and a collector's eye for detail. The work moves between screens and systems, building websites, interfaces, and small worlds that feel intuitive and lasting.

Raised between the Northern Italian province of Padova and Los Angeles County, with Turkish and Mexican roots, Jasmine grew up learning from other cultures, and that curiosity shapes the studio's work. She studied mathematics and computational cognitive science at UC Davis and has spent over four years building software. Outside of that, she tinkers, reads, games with friends, and curates personal art projects.

What's next

The studio is growing in two directions. The first is web design for independent, aesthetically driven businesses, where the site should feel as intentional as the space it represents. The second is play: a Roblox game in development with friends, bringing the same attention to atmosphere and detail into an interactive world.
